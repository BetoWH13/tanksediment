Water Heater Maintenance Context Tool

Use a few known facts to organize your maintenance context. This tool does not calculate how much sediment is inside your heater, predict efficiency loss, diagnose a problem, or tell you that service is required.

Why the change? Sediment inside a specific water heater cannot be reliably estimated from age and water-hardness inputs alone. Heater design, local chemistry, temperature, usage, prior maintenance, and tank condition all matter.

Your information

What this tool can actually tell you

It can calculate elapsed time from dates you provide and flag factors that may justify checking the manufacturer's maintenance guidance or asking a qualified professional for an assessment.

It deliberately avoids millimeter estimates, efficiency percentages, fixed risk scores, and universal flushing schedules.

Your maintenance context

Before You Act

Older or neglected water heaters can behave differently when disturbed. If you are unsure of the heater's condition, fuel type, shutoff procedure, drain-valve condition, or manufacturer instructions, consider professional assessment before attempting maintenance.

If you use the service number on this site, you may be connected with an independent third-party provider or service network. Tank Sediment is not the plumbing company performing the work.
