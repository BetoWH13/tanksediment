How to Flush a Tankless Water Heater

Tankless maintenance is model-specific. This page explains the decision, not a universal flushing procedure. Service-port layout, approved descaling solution, isolation steps, circulation time, restart procedure, and maintenance interval can differ by manufacturer and model.

Mineral scale can develop inside a tankless heater, especially where water is hard. Whether descaling is needed, and how often, depends on the unit, water chemistry, usage, prior maintenance, and the manufacturer’s instructions.

When descaling may be worth investigating

  • The manufacturer specifies periodic descaling for your model.
  • Your home has hard water and the unit has a history of scale-related service.
  • Flow or temperature performance has changed and a technician has ruled out other causes.
  • The unit displays a maintenance or scale-related code described in its manual.

Why we do not publish one universal procedure

Tankless units may use gas or high-voltage electricity, internal valves, service ports, pumps, filters, sensors, and heat exchangers arranged differently from one model to another. A generic sequence can be wrong for a specific appliance.

Stop and use qualified service if you smell gas, see damaged wiring, leaking fittings, corrosion, deteriorated service valves, or if you cannot positively identify the model-specific isolation and restart procedure.

What to check before any maintenance

  • Exact brand and model number.
  • Owner or service manual instructions for descaling.
  • Approved cleaning solution and concentration.
  • Whether service/isolation valves are actually installed.
  • Any warranty conditions tied to maintenance.
  • Your measured water hardness, if available.

How often should a tankless heater be descaled?

There is no single schedule that fits every installation. Follow the manufacturer’s interval for your model and adjust only when a qualified professional or documented water-quality conditions justify it. Hardness can increase scale potential, but it does not by itself determine an exact calendar interval.

About water softeners and filters

A correctly selected ion-exchange softener can reduce hardness minerals and therefore reduce scale potential, but it is not automatically appropriate for every home or appliance. Sediment filters address suspended particles; they do not remove dissolved hardness minerals. See our water softener context guide for the distinction.
