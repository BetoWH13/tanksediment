Tankless heater losing performance

Scale is one possibility. Model, error codes, water hardness, and other faults matter too.

Tankless service context →
Hard water or visible scale

Hardness raises scale potential, but it does not diagnose every heater problem.

Understand hardness →

Minerals and other material can settle inside tank-style water heaters over time. How much accumulates, how quickly it happens, and whether it is affecting a particular heater depend on local water chemistry, heater design, usage, age, and maintenance history.

Tank Sediment is an informational site. We do not inspect your heater and we cannot determine its internal condition from a webpage.

Maintenance context tool: Use the Water Heater Maintenance Context Tool to organize heater age, last service, water hardness, and what you are observing. It does not estimate a physical amount of sediment inside the tank.
